Recommended Formatting Guidelines
Font: Times New Roman, 12 pt for body text.
Chapter headings: 16 pt, bold; subheadings: 14 pt, bold.
Line spacing: 1.5; paragraph alignment: justified.
Margins: 1 inch on all sides, unless departmental guidelines specify otherwise.
Page numbering: Roman numerals for preliminary pages and Arabic numerals for the main chapters.
Use a consistent reference style (IEEE or APA) as required by the department.
